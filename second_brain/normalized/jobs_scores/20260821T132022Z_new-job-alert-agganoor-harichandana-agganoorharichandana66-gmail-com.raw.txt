**Quantum computing in plain language**

Think of a classical computer as a very fast, very organized librarian.  
The librarian can look up a book in a stack of books that are either *on* (1) or *off* (0).  
Every instruction the computer follows is like the librarian turning a page, moving a book, or flipping a switch.  
All of the librarian’s work is deterministic: if you give the same instruction twice, the result is always the same.
