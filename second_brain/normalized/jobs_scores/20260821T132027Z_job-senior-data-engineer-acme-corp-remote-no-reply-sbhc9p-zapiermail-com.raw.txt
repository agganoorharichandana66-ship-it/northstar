### Quantum computing in plain language

| **Classical computer** | **Quantum computer** |
|------------------------|----------------------|
| Uses **bits** that are either **0** or **1** | Uses **qubits** that can be **0, 1, or both at once** |
| Does one calculation at a time (though many processors can work in parallel) | Can explore many calculations *simultaneously* because of superposition |
| Logic is built with AND, OR, NOT gates | Logic is built with quantum gates that manipulate probability amplitudes |
| To get an answer you read the final bit(s) | To get an answer you measure the qubits, collapsing the probabilities into a single outcome |
