### What is a “quantum computer”?

A normal computer (the one you use for typing, browsing, playing games, etc.) stores information in **bits**.  
A bit can be either **0** or **1**—think of a light switch that’s either OFF or ON.

A quantum computer stores information in **qubits**.  
A qubit is a bit that can be 0, 1, **or both at the same time** (a superposition).  
Because of this, a quantum computer can hold and process many possibilities simultaneously.
