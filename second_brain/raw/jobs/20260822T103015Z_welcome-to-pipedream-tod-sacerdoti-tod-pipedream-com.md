    Want to get the most out of <strong>String</strong>? Quality prompts make a huge difference.
    <br /><br />

    Here are our best practices from reviewing thousands of user prompts:
    <br />
<ul>
   <li> <strong>Be clear on the trigger:</strong>
    String builds event-based agents. That means your prompt needs to describe what makes the agent start doing its job.
    Does it run every day at 8am Pacific or when you receive a new email in Gmail? Be specific.</li>

    <li><strong>Be clear on the apps:</strong> "When I get a new email" --> "When I get a new email <strong>in Gmail</strong>"</li>

    <li><strong>Break it down:</strong>
    String can build complex agents that perform tasks across many tools. But it’s best to focus on building one task at a time.
    </li>
	</ul>
